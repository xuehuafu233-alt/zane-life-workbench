# Start with a job choice

## Describe the choice

```text
Use zane-workbench.
Job A pays more but has a long commute. Job B pays less and offers more flexibility.
I want stable finances and time with my family. Here are the offers and my expenses.
Help me compare the two.
```

The Agent considers income, necessary spending, time, and family arrangements, then explains which conditions matter most to the choice.

## Put each option into a real week

Ask: “Show me a workable weekly schedule and budget for each option.” Adjust the tradeoffs to match what you care about: “I would accept less income for two free evenings each week.”

## Bring back new information

If Job A adds two remote days per week, share that update. Revisit commuting time, family time, and the original recommendation.

## Continue later

Ask the Agent to save the comparison, your decision, and the next step in your workspace. Give the next session that folder. In a regular chat, save a continuation note and paste it back with the relevant material.

The same approach works for moving, learning, family arrangements, and personal projects.
